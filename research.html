Research & Innovation

Turning agronomic science into practical solutions that work in farmers' fields.

Focus areas

Where our research makes a difference

Our R&D programme targets the biggest challenges farmers face today — rising input costs, declining soil health, pest resistance and climate stress.

Nutrient-Use Efficiency

Chelated, coated and nano-scale nutrient technologies that deliver more to the plant and lose less to the environment.

Soil Health

Microbial consortia, humic substances and organic carbon builders that restore soil structure and biology.

Bio-based Crop Protection

Botanical extracts and beneficial microbes that complement chemistry in integrated pest management.

Climate Resilience

Bio-stimulants that help crops tolerate heat, drought and salinity stress.

Formulation Science

Stable, easy-to-mix formulations — SC, WG, SL and liquid fertilizers — with improved shelf life.

Precision Application

Products and protocols optimised for drip fertigation and drone spraying.

Our process

From lab to land

  1. Discover

    Identify field problems through farmer feedback, agronomist reports and soil data.

  2. Formulate

    Develop and refine prototypes in our formulation and analytical laboratory.

  3. Validate

    Test efficacy, crop safety and stability in greenhouse and multi-location field trials.

  4. Deliver

    Register, launch and support the product with demonstrations and training.
