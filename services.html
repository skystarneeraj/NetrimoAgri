Services

Expert agronomy support from soil testing to harvest — because the right product needs the right advice.

What we offer

Agronomy services that grow with you

Our field team supports farmers, dealers and institutions with expert guidance from soil to harvest.

Soil & Leaf Testing

Lab analysis of macro- and micronutrients, pH, EC and organic carbon, with an easy-to-read report and fertilizer recommendation.

Crop Advisory

Stage-wise nutrition and protection schedules, pest and disease diagnosis, and seasonal advisories by phone, WhatsApp or field visit.

Farmer Training

Village meetings, field days and workshops on balanced nutrition, IPM and safe handling of crop protection products.

Field Demonstrations

Side-by-side demonstration plots so farmers can see results on their own crops before they buy.

Drone Spraying Support

Guidance on drone-compatible products, dosage and water volumes, through trained service partners.

Dealer & Distributor Support

Reliable supply, point-of-sale material, product training and technical support for channel partners.

How it works

Getting started is simple

  1. Request
