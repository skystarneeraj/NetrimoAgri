About Nutrimo Agri Science

Protecting crops, nurturing soil and helping farmers grow — with science they can trust.

Our story

Built to protect crops and help farmers grow

Nutrimo Agri Science was founded with a simple belief: farmers deserve inputs that are effective, honest and backed by science. What began as a focused range of crop nutrition products has grown into a complete portfolio of fertilizers, crop protection solutions, bio-stimulants and organic inputs.

Today we work alongside farmers, dealers and agronomists to improve soil health, raise yields and reduce the environmental footprint of agriculture — one field at a time.

Our emblem

The green leaf shield represents crop protection and care for the land. The rising sun stands for growth, energy and a brighter future for every farming family.

Mission, vision and values

Our Mission

To deliver research-driven crop nutrition and protection solutions that improve farm productivity and profitability while safeguarding soil, water and biodiversity.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted agri-science partner for farmers — known for quality, innovation and a genuine commitment to sustainable agriculture.

Our journey

Milestones

A steady path of growth built on trust, quality and innovation.

  1. 2015

    Foundation

    Nutrimo Agri Science is established with a focused range of water-soluble fertilizers.

  2. 2018

    Crop protection launch

    Portfolio expands into insecticides, fungicides and herbicides.

  3. 2021

    R&D centre

    In-house formulation and quality-control laboratory commissioned.

  4. 2024

    Bio-solutions division

    Launch of bio-fertilizers, bio-stimulants and organic inputs.

  5. 2026

    Growing network

    Expanding dealer network and digital farmer advisory services.
