Knowledge hub

Quick tips from our agronomists

Nutrition

Why balanced NPK beats “more urea”

Excess nitrogen makes crops lush but weak, attracting sucking pests and causing lodging. A soil test-based NPK ratio, plus sulphur and zinc where deficient, gives stronger plants and better grain fill.

Protection

Rotate modes of action

Spraying the same insecticide or fungicide group again and again builds resistance. Alternate products with different modes of action and spray only when pests cross the economic threshold.

Soil health

Feed the soil, not just the plant

Adding organic matter and bio-fertilizers improves water holding, nutrient availability and root growth — making every bag of fertilizer more effective.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I mix water-soluble fertilizers with pesticides?

Many water-soluble fertilizers are compatible with common pesticides, but not all combinations are. Always do a jar test first, never mix calcium-based fertilizers with phosphates or sulphates, and follow label instructions. Ask our agronomists if unsure.

How often should I test my soil?

We recommend a soil test at least once every two to three years per field, and before each season for high-value horticulture crops.

Where can I buy Nutrimo products?

Our products are available through authorised dealers. Contact us with your district and we'll connect you with the nearest dealer.

Are Safety Data Sheets (SDS) available?

Yes. SDS and technical data sheets are available for every product on request — use the inquiry form and mention the product name.

Are bio-fertilizers safe to use with chemical fertilizers?

Yes, bio-fertilizers work well alongside balanced chemical nutrition. Avoid mixing them directly with fungicides or bactericides, and apply them a few days apart.

Request product labels, SDS, technical data sheets or crop-specific schedules.
