Plant health guide

Spot nutrient deficiency early

Leaves and stems tell you what a crop is missing. Use this guide to identify the symptom — then pick the matching Nutrimo solution.

  • N

    Nitrogen

    Yellowing of older lower leaves, weak stem, reduced growth.

    Try: NPK 19:19:19, NPK 20:20:20
  • P

    Phosphorus

    Dark green to purple leaves, stunted growth, delayed maturity.

    Try: NPK 12:61:00, NPK 00:52:34
  • K

    Potassium

    Brown, scorched leaf margins, weakened resistance.

    Try: NPK 00:00:50, NPK 13:00:45
  • Zn

    Zinc

    Small leaves, shortened internodes, interveinal yellowing.

    Try: Zinc Sulphate, Micro Ultra
  • Fe

    Iron

    Yellow young leaves with green veins; severe cases show whitish leaves.

    Try: Vardaan, Vardaan Gold
  • Mg

    Magnesium

    Interveinal chlorosis on older leaves, premature leaf drop.

    Try: Master
  • Ca

    Calcium

    Deformed young leaves, blossom end rot, poor root growth.

    Try: Chamatkar, Camsu-B
  • B

    Boron

    Cracked stems, poor flowering, death of growing points.

    Try: Camsu-B, Micro Ultra
